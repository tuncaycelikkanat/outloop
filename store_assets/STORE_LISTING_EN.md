## Full Description (Max 4000 Characters)
Break free from infinite video doomscrolling and reclaim your focus.

Outloop is a 100% offline, privacy-first digital wellbeing app designed to block infinite Instagram Reels while keeping your normal feed, stories, direct messages (DMs), and profile completely usable.

WHY OUTLOOP?
Ever opened Instagram for a quick message and lost an hour to mindless short videos? Outloop breaks the dopamine trap, returning control over your attention to you.

KEY FEATURES:
• Smart Reels Blocking: Instantly detects when Reels tab or short-video viewers are opened and gently navigates you back to safety.
• Mindful Gateway: Instead of a harsh exit, pause with a gentle breathing circle or a 10-second reflection barrier to stop impulsive scrolling.
• Flexible DM Protection: Watch a single reel sent by a friend in Direct Messages, but prevent doomscrolling to the next recommended video.
• Daily Quota & Heatmap: Set your custom daily allowance and monitor your focus trends with a sleek activity heatmap.
• Bedtime Mode: Automatically strengthens protection during nighttime hours so your sleep remains undisturbed.
• Willpower Lock: Protect your intentions with a PIN code or arithmetic challenge before disabling protection.
• Zero Ads, Zero Trackers: Fully independent, ad-free, and respectful of your attention.

PRIVACY & SECURITY GUARANTEE (100% OFFLINE):
• NO INTERNET PERMISSION: Outloop does not request `android.permission.INTERNET`. Not a single byte can ever leave your phone.
• NO ACCOUNTS REQUIRED: No sign-ups, no logins, no telemetry.
• ZERO DATA COLLECTION: Screen text, personal messages, passwords, and private content are never captured, logged, or stored.

ACCESSIBILITY SERVICE DECLARATION:
Outloop uses the Android AccessibilityService API strictly to detect when Instagram Reels are being displayed on screen and perform the navigation action to exit the video player. It does not monitor any other apps, read your personal messages, or transmit any data whatsoever.
